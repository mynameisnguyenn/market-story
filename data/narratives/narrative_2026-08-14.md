a) **Fiscal credibility trumps inflation for credit pricing** — the "US Treasury reveals America is insolvent" headline (Yahoo Finance 12:30), combined with Social Security COLA potentially 3.6% for 2027, represents the credit market's structural concern: lower inflation doesn't fix the deficit, and the deficit is the thing that will eventually cause credit stress. The 2bps post-CPI widening is a signal that credit is ahead of equities on this regime shift.

b) **AI capex destruction is an independent credit driver** — GOOGL FCF negative, META under pressure, AMZN derating through the GOOGL template. These mega-cap capex programs represent $100s of billions of corporate debt capacity. As the market re-prices whether this spend is value-creating, credit markets front-run the eventual refinancing risk.

c) **Calibration noise** — 2bps might be one-day variation, not a regime shift. The Aug 12 FRED vintage (the full CPI-day reaction) is the settling point. If the Aug 12 vintage reverses to 2.69%, the Aug 11 widening was noise. If it confirms at 2.72%+, the bull gate is formally uncleared post-CPI.

The weight of evidence favors (a) + (b): two independent readings (US Treasury insolvency headline + AI capex derating) both pointing to credit concerns that are structural, not data-dependent. The 2bps is consistent with early-stage regime adjustment, not noise.

**2. Iran-BRICS: the diplomatic offramp just narrowed significantly.**

Iran is not joining the BRICS NDB to have leverage in a near-term US deal — it's joining to make itself sanctions-proof at a structural level. The signal for the Hormuz crisis: the "carrot" of sanctions relief (the core of any US-Iran deal) loses its value if Iran can finance its economy through BRICS channels. The bull gate that requires WTI below $78 via an Iran deal just got more expensive to clear.

The demand-destruction path to $78 (EIA builds + economic slowdown) remains available but is a different signal. WTI at $78 via demand destruction = recession insurance needed, not "all-clear" extension.
