**2. Semiconductor derating is systematically worse than platform derating.**

Platform derating (GOOGL, AMZN, CRM) hits companies that can pivot — they have diversified revenues and management can cut costs. Semiconductor derating (ASML, TSMC) hits capital-intensive infrastructure with 3–5 year capex commitments, no cost-flex, and sovereign government involvement (Taiwan, Netherlands, US). ASML's −6.89% is the Dutch national champion's single worst day in months. TSMC's −4.98% is a Taiwan geopolitical and AI capex signal simultaneously. Goldman Sachs calling out AI valuations publicly is the catalyst for institutional algo de-risking — when GS speaks, risk parity models listen.

**3. WTI below $73: 60-day expiration, Iran diplomacy at risk.**

The $73 floor break is clean but not catastrophic — WTI at 72.58 is still within the EIA-fundamental range (crude draws −8,263 MBBL). The risk is the 60-day expiration of the sweeping oil waiver. If Iran does not deliver nuclear inspection access (BBC: "no new commitments"), the US administration faces political pressure to rescind. The oil market is pricing supply certainty NOW; it is NOT pricing the 60-day renewal risk. At day 60 (late August), if the Iran deal collapses, WTI snapback to $80+ would arrive with a September FOMC that can't cut on oil-driven inflation — the worst macro combination.

**4. The dollar at 99.6th %ile — the ticking correlation bomb.**

DXY at 101.36 (99.6th %ile, z=+2.96) is the most stretched cross-asset level in the brief.
