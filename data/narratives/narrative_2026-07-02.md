## Risk lens

**1. NFP Thursday + July 4 holiday = trapped risk.** ADP +98k. If NFP <130k Thursday, the equity market can't react Friday (US holiday). Monday July 7 opens with three days of undigested payroll shock, weekend carry positioning, and potential BoJ Friday-night action all compounding. **2. VRP −1.0: vol regime warning.** Negative VRP historically resolves via: (a) realized vol mean-reverts lower — benign; or (b) implied vol catches up violently — dangerous. In the current environment (credit at 14th %ile, 10Y at 89th %ile, ADP miss), path (b) dominates. Options markets at VIX 16.3 are priced as if nothing is happening while rates stress, credit deceleration, and payroll risk are all live.

**3. 10Y at 89th %ile, VIX at 30th %ile: divergence cannot persist.** Historically, rates-stressed / equity-vol-complacent environments resolve through equity vol catching up, not rates normalizing. Warsh's "no rate hints" kills the rate-relief scenario. Thursday payrolls become the convergence trigger.

The three-vector Monday July 7 risk: NFP shock + USD/JPY weekend move + CFTC Nasdaq positioning (June 30 vintage due July 7) all target the same crowded names simultaneously.
